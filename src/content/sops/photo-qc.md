## QC before anything goes out

- Every edited frame gets an automatic QC pass with a score and a list of issues. It never hides or rejects a frame on its own.
- A person opens the shoot, looks at every frame, ticks the issues they agree with and fixes them: download for editing, then upload the corrected finals by file name.
- **Publish** makes the approved frames visible to the client. Nothing reaches the client before that.
- Frames moved to internal by hand stay internal on every later publish.
