## Changing a shoot time

| Who moves it | How | What the client gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The client | From the portal, inside the open times | A note that the new time waits for confirmation |
| The team | Reschedule on the booking | The time email with the new calendar file |
| Weather or access | Reschedule, with a note on the booking | The time email |

- A client's move shows on the booking as "Moved by the client" until someone confirms the new time.
- Canceling a booking voids an unpaid invoice. A paid one is refunded by hand in the payment processor.
