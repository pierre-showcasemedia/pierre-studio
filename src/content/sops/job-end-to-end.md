## The job in seven steps

1. **Booking.** The client books on the website at /book. The booking lands in the studio on its own (Admin, Bookings) with the client, the listing and the invoice, priced from the same price table as the website.
2. **Confirm.** Confirm the time within the hour during business hours (Confirm time on the booking). The client gets the confirmation email with a calendar file.
3. **Shooter.** Assign a shooter on the booking. They get the calendar invite with the shoot brief.
4. **Shoot and upload.** The shooter uploads from the card on Upload a shoot. The raw files never reach the client.
5. **Edit and QC.** Editing runs, then every frame gets an automatic QC pass. A person reviews, fixes and publishes. See [Photo QC](/admin/sops/photo-qc).
6. **Deliver.** A person sends the delivery email from the listing. Delivery is due {{turnaround}} after the shoot. See [Delivery](/admin/sops/delivery).
7. **Paid.** The gallery unlocks when the invoice is paid. See [Payments](/admin/sops/payments).

> [!note] Times
> Every date and time in the studio is {{timeZone}}.
