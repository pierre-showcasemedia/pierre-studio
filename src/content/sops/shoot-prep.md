## Before the shoot

The shoot brief on the calendar invite has what the shooter needs: the address, the time, the services and add-ons, access notes, the client's notes and anything the team added.

- Read it the night before. Call the business line, {{phone}}, about anything missing.
- Upload the card the same day, from Upload a shoot. Raw files go straight to editing; nothing reaches the client before QC.
- Flag anything the client should hear about (access, a room you couldn't shoot) on the booking.
