## Delivering a gallery

- Deliver within {{turnaround}} of the shoot.
- After Publish, preview the delivery email on the listing and send it. Delivery emails are never automatic.
- The portal gives full-size and MLS-size downloads, a watermarked share link for sellers and co-agents, revision requests and an approve button.
- **Payment unlocks downloads.** Unpaid at delivery, the gallery stays watermarked; the client can still share the preview.
- A client missing files in Download all: press **Rebuild zips** on the listing.
