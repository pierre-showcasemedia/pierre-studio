## The booking form

The website's form at /book asks for the size of the home, a package or a service on its own, add-ons, the area, a date and time of day, access notes and the client's details, then the terms.

- The website and the studio price from the same table. A booking whose total differs, or that has a quoted line, waits as Needs quote.
- The studio confirms nothing on its own: the client gets a "we have your request" email, and a person confirms the time.
- A price change goes in the website's price table and the studio's copy together. See [Website changes](/admin/sops/website-changes).
