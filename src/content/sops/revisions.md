## Revisions

- The client asks from the gallery, naming the exact photos. The request lands in Revisions with every file attached.
- Write up what needs to change, fix it, upload the corrected files by name, and publish. They land in the gallery's Revised section.
- Reply to the client from the revision with what changed.
- Anything that isn't a fix (new photos, more rooms) is a new booking or an added item, never a free revision.
