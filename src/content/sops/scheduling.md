## Scheduling

- One calendar event per shoot, made by the studio when the time is confirmed. The event carries the client notes and the access notes the shooter needs.
- Shoots run inside the business hours set in the studio's settings. The client's reschedule picker only offers open times.
- Assign the shooter as soon as the time is set. Every booking for the next business day should have a named shooter by the end of the day before.
- A second visit to the same property goes on the booking as an additional shoot. See [Return shoots](/admin/sops/return-shoots).
