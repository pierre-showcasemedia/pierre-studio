## How a booking is paid

- Every booking gets an invoice when it comes in. With card payments the client pays the invoice online; with other ways to pay, a person marks it paid when the money arrives.
- **Only a confirmed payment unlocks the gallery:** the processor's paid event, or an admin's Mark paid. Never a page the client lands on. Watermarked previews stay visible the whole time.
- Quoted bookings wait as Needs quote until someone sets the amount.

## A stuck payment

1. Open the booking. The invoice card shows the processor's last event.
2. Press **Check now** to read the invoice from the processor again.
3. Still stuck: Mark paid only once the money is really there, and note how it was confirmed.

> [!note] Amounts
> Money shows only in the admin, the payments channel and the reports. The team hub never carries an amount.
