# Reviewing your new site (for Pierre)

Your new website is on a private preview address in your Vercel account
(Vercel, team Showcase Media LLC, project **pierre-site**, the **Visit**
button). Your clients still see your current site. Nothing changes for them
until you say it's ready.

Take your time. Write down anything you'd like changed, big or small, and send
the list to Hammad in one email. Please don't edit the site yourself during the
review, so we're never both changing the same thing.

## 1. The pages (about 20 minutes)

Open each page on your computer and on your phone.

- **Home.** Your new video, then the four boxes under it (a first draft:
  send your own wording), the short introduction, a mix of portfolio photos,
  services, a featured property, About, testimonials and how booking works.
- **Portfolio.** One gallery with every kind of shot mixed together, and your
  films below it. Click any photo to see it larger.
- **Services.** Each service with its description. "See examples" opens the
  portfolio (or the films for video).
- **About.** About Showcase Media, with you as the founder.
- **Contact.** Your phone, email, address and social links.
- **The footer** at the bottom of every page: "Created by Showcase Media. All
  Rights Reserved."

Things to look for: wording that isn't how you'd say it, anything that
could mislead a client, photos you'd swap, and prices.

## 2. A test booking (about 10 minutes)

Open **Book a Shoot** and go all the way through with your second email
address. Write TEST in the notes.

- As a real estate agent, the brokerage is required.
- "How big is the property?" has the finished size, acreage notes and a plat
  or lot map link.
- The free floor plan note says it covers up to 3,000 sq ft of total scanned
  space. In the add-ons, "Larger floor plan" lets a client pick 3,001 to
  5,000 ($30), 5,001 to 7,500 ($50) or 7,501 to 10,000 ($75).
- Drone photos can be added to the Highlight Walkthrough Bundle. Twilight
  photography sits right above virtual twilight.
- "How do we get in?", "Vacant or occupied?" and "Notes and special
  requests".
- The "Good to know" list on the last step.

Check every price against your price sheet. If the booking form's settings are
in (step 5 of your setup), the request reaches your Formspree inbox, and your
studio once it's switched on. If they aren't yet, the form shows a test
message and sends nothing. Both are fine for now.

## 3. What we still need from you

- Your brokerage logos, for the "Trusted by" strip.
- Two or three floor plan examples.
- Links to a few of your Matterport and Zillow 3D tours.
- Business portrait examples (from your old site is fine).

- Your wording for the four boxes under the video, and any page wording you'd

- The 40% version of the hero video, if you'd like to compare it. Both links

Once you're live, Hammad will show you how to change your own wording and
photos. Your contact details, services, testimonials and the home page video
